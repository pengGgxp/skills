# CNBlogs Platform Profile

Platform ID: `cnblogs`

## Position

- Purpose: durable developer notes, implementation records, and technical tutorials.
- Reader intent: follow an engineering thought process and reuse its practical result.
- Best fit: development logs, deep tutorials, architecture reflections, and lessons from debugging.

## Write

- Title: name the engineering subject and concrete lesson or task.
- Opening: establish the project context, constraints, and why the solution was needed.
- Structure: context, options considered, implementation, code or configuration, verification, and retrospective lessons.
- Voice: thoughtful developer-to-developer writing with room for reasoning and honest tradeoffs.
- Depth: preserve code, environment, failed approaches, and the rationale behind the final choice.
- Links: include official references and relevant source code without turning the article into a link list.
- Ending: capture what should be reused and what was specific to this project.

## Visuals

- Prefer diagrams, traces, code-adjacent screenshots, and before-and-after runtime evidence.
- Ensure wide technical images remain readable in the site's content column.

## Avoid

- Repackaging a generic Blog article without developer context, hiding failed attempts, and optimizing headings only for search.
- Unexplained snippets or conclusions that cannot be traced to the implementation.

## Platform QA

- Confirm the development narrative remains technically useful rather than merely chronological.
- Verify code, environment, tradeoffs, failure analysis, and final validation.

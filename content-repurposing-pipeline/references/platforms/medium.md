# Medium Platform Profile

Platform ID: `medium`

## Position

- Purpose: English-language long-form explanation, reflection, and technical storytelling for an international audience.
- Reader intent: understand an idea or experience beyond a local context and follow a coherent argument.
- Best fit: engineering lessons, AI workflows, product thinking, case studies, and reflective tutorials.

## Write

- Title: make one clear English promise or question; prefer natural editorial phrasing over literal translation.
- Opening: establish the human or technical stakes and define unfamiliar regional context immediately.
- Structure: strong lead, context, thesis, evidence or walkthrough, implications, limits, and conclusion.
- Voice: idiomatic English, thoughtful, specific, and accessible across regions.
- Depth: preserve technical rigor while explaining China-specific products, institutions, terms, and assumptions for an international reader.
- Links: cite primary sources and add a canonical-source note when appropriate and supported by the publishing workflow.
- Ending: state the broader lesson and one natural discussion or follow-up prompt.

## Visuals

- Use a strong lead image and clean inline visuals with English captions and alt text.
- Replace or annotate screenshots whose untranslated UI prevents understanding, without fabricating the interface.

## Avoid

- Sentence-by-sentence translation, unexplained local context, imported Chinese rhetorical patterns, generic global claims, and invented cultural comparisons.
- Assuming English readers know the original platform, product, or policy environment.

## Platform QA

- Use a reviewer capable of idiomatic English editing as well as factual review.
- Check terminology, regional context, translation fidelity, citations, image comprehension, and current publication constraints.

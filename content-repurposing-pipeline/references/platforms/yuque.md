# Yuque Platform Profile

Platform ID: `yuque`

## Position

- Purpose: maintainable knowledge-base documentation that can grow and be linked over time.
- Reader intent: locate a specific concept or procedure and understand how it relates to a larger body of knowledge.
- Best fit: series, internal or personal manuals, learning systems, reference material, and maintained tutorials.

## Write

- Title: use a stable topic or task name rather than a campaign-style headline.
- Opening: define purpose, audience, scope, prerequisites, and the document's place in the knowledge base.
- Structure: overview, terminology, prerequisites, hierarchical procedures or concepts, examples, troubleshooting, and related documents.
- Voice: neutral, maintainable, and explicit about definitions and dependencies.
- Depth: preserve details readers need to execute or maintain the subject; separate beginner paths from advanced notes.
- Links: use descriptive cross-links and avoid unexplained orphan references.
- Ending: point to related documents, ownership, or the next maintained topic rather than requesting engagement.

## Visuals

- Prefer diagrams, annotated screenshots, tables, and stable process visuals that remain useful after repeated reference.
- Add captions and alt text that explain the information, not merely the appearance.

## Avoid

- Social-media hooks, promotional CTA language, unexplained hierarchy, duplicated sections across documents, and time-sensitive claims without context.
- Converting a one-off post into a knowledge-base page without restructuring it.

## Platform QA

- Confirm navigation, hierarchy, definitions, prerequisites, cross-links, and maintenance-sensitive claims.
- Check that the page remains useful when reached directly rather than through a feed.

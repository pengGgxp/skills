# Bilibili Platform Profile

Platform ID: `bilibili`

## Position

- Purpose: a video production package for explanation, demonstration, and audience retention.
- Reader intent: watch a result develop, see the real workflow, and understand enough to reproduce or evaluate it.
- Best fit: product demonstrations, tutorials, experiments, technical explainers, and build stories.

## Write

- Title: state the visible result, problem, or experiment honestly and make the subject recognizable.
- Opening: use a short cold open that shows the outcome or central tension before background.
- Structure: hook, promise, setup, chaptered explanation or demo, verification, limitations, recap, and one CTA.
- Voice: natural spoken language with sentences that can be read aloud; explain jargon before it appears on screen.
- Depth: keep technical details that affect the demonstration, and move dense reference material into on-screen text, description, or linked resources.
- Links: prepare a concise description with verified resources and any necessary disclosure.
- Ending: recap the result and invite one relevant follow-up action.

## Visuals And Production

- Write `index.md` as a production package with sections for narration, screen action, evidence to show, asset path, and optional edit note.
- Bind every product claim to an authentic screen recording or runtime capture; generated visuals may only explain or brand.
- Plan readable captions, cursor focus, zooms, pauses, and sensitive-data masking.

## Avoid

- Reading an article verbatim, long talking-head setup before value, fake interface demonstrations, unsupported performance claims, and visual actions that do not match narration.
- Treating a thumbnail as evidence of product behavior.

## Platform QA

- Read the narration aloud and verify timing, chapter logic, screen actions, evidence, captions, and asset availability.
- Keep the version blocked when a required demonstration shot cannot be produced or inspected.

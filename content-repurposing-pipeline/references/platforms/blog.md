# Blog Platform Profile

Platform ID: `blog`

## Position

- Purpose: canonical long-form publication on an owned site.
- Reader intent: search, learn thoroughly, return later, or cite the article.
- Best fit: tutorials, technical explanations, case studies, product walkthroughs, and durable reflections.

## Write

- Title: state the concrete problem, object, or outcome with natural search language; keep it readable rather than keyword-stuffed.
- Opening: define the situation, why it matters, and what the reader will be able to do or understand.
- Structure: use a complete argument or reproducible sequence, then include evidence, limitations, and a practical conclusion.
- Voice: precise, authorial, calm, and willing to explain context.
- Depth: retain useful commands, code, versions, edge cases, and implementation detail.
- Links: use descriptive inline links when they help; do not force a public references section when the article reads better without one.
- Ending: summarize the durable lesson and offer one relevant next step or related article.

## Visuals

- Use a useful lead image plus screenshots, diagrams, or comparisons at the exact point they support.
- Check both desktop and phone rendering, especially code, captions, GIFs, and wide screenshots.
- Let readers inspect a full-resolution demonstration when the inline crop omits relevant context.

## Avoid

- Generic SEO introductions, repeated summaries, keyword variants, decorative stock images, and raw note dumps.
- Assuming the reader saw an earlier social post or platform version.

## Platform QA

- Confirm the article works as the canonical standalone version.
- Verify heading hierarchy, code formatting, link destinations, image responsiveness, and mobile legibility.

# Common Platform Contract

Use this contract with exactly one selected platform profile. These profiles are internal editorial defaults, not claims about official platform policy.

## Load And Route

1. Read this file before adapting or reviewing any platform version.
2. Read only `references/platforms/<platform>.md` for the exact selected platform ID.
3. If that profile is missing, record a blocker and stop the platform adaptation until a profile exists.
4. Keep each platform version independent. Do not overwrite or mechanically rename another platform version.

## Preserve Source Truth

- Preserve the approved master's facts, evidence strength, uncertainty, commands, versions, and material limitations.
- Reorder, compress, expand explanation, or omit secondary detail only when it improves destination fit.
- Do not invent personal experience, results, quotations, popularity, metrics, endorsements, or platform-specific claims.
- Keep the platform version understandable without requiring the reader to open the master.
- Recheck any claim affected by time, software versions, policy, pricing, or product behavior.

## Adapt Deliberately

- Define one reader, one reading situation, one content promise, and one primary action.
- Rewrite the title, opening, hierarchy, paragraph rhythm, examples, links, visuals, and ending for the destination.
- Prefer concrete language and visible evidence over generic enthusiasm.
- Keep useful detail; remove repetition, internal workflow notes, review instructions, and template scaffolding.
- Treat suggested lengths and structures as editorial defaults, not quotas.

## Visual Contract

- Follow `references/visual-assets-and-agent-review.md`.
- Give every selected image a specific role in the surrounding section.
- Require an authentic runtime capture for a real product, tool, CLI, app, service, or hardware workflow.
- Inspect legibility at the destination's normal phone or desktop reading width.
- Adapt crop, ordering, captions, and cover composition for the selected platform instead of reusing assets blindly.
- Preserve source, rights, privacy, local path, and alt-text checks.

## Links And Calls To Action

- Use links only when the destination supports them and they help the reader.
- Do not disguise advertising, affiliate links, or unsupported recommendations as editorial advice.
- Use at most one primary call to action. Make it natural for the platform and proportional to the article.
- Never require engagement, manufacture urgency, or promise a result the article cannot support.

## Volatile Platform Constraints

Before publication, verify current official or in-product constraints for title and body limits, image and video formats, link behavior, tags, Markdown or HTML support, disclosure requirements, and prohibited content. Do not preserve guessed numeric limits in these profiles. If a current constraint changes the reviewed artifact, revise it and return that revision to platform review.

## Platform QA

The independent platform reviewer must confirm:

- the selected profile was followed intentionally;
- the version is meaningfully adapted rather than synonym-swapped;
- the opening delivers the platform-specific promise quickly;
- structure, voice, depth, links, visuals, and CTA fit the destination;
- facts and uncertainty still match the approved master;
- screenshots prove the exact nearby product claim;
- no template notes, review instructions, private data, or unsupported claims remain;
- current publishing constraints have either been verified or are recorded as a pre-publication task.

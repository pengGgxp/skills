# Zhihu Platform Profile

Platform ID: `zhihu`

## Position

- Purpose: answer a meaningful question through reasoning, evidence, and experience.
- Reader intent: compare explanations, understand tradeoffs, or decide what to believe or do.
- Best fit: methods, technical explanations, informed opinions, comparisons, and lessons from real practice.

## Write

- Title: formulate or preserve the question the article actually answers; avoid pretending a broad topic has one universal answer.
- Opening: give the thesis or short answer first, then state scope and assumptions.
- Structure: develop reasons, evidence, examples, alternatives, counterpoints, and limits before the final synthesis.
- Voice: clear, evidence-led, intellectually honest, and personal only when experience is real and relevant.
- Depth: explain why, not only how; distinguish direct evidence, inference, and opinion.
- Links: cite useful primary or authoritative sources inline when claims benefit from verification.
- Ending: state who should use the advice, who should not, and what tradeoff remains.

## Visuals

- Prefer diagrams, comparisons, data, or authentic demonstrations that advance the reasoning.
- Caption the inference a reader may draw and avoid images used only as visual breaks.

## Avoid

- Fake credentials, invented consensus, rhetorical certainty, answer-first clickbait with no reasoning, and irrelevant autobiography.
- Turning a product introduction into an undisclosed promotion.

## Platform QA

- Confirm the version answers a recognizable question and supports its thesis.
- Check source quality, counterarguments, uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and the line between evidence and opinion.

# Xiaohongshu Platform Profile

Platform ID: `xiaohongshu`

## Position

- Purpose: discovery, fast comprehension, saving, and experience exchange.
- Reader intent: decide quickly whether a method or tool is relevant and capture actionable steps.
- Best fit: tool notes, workflows, checklists, learning methods, before-and-after comparisons, and concise tutorials.

## Write

- Title: lead with a concrete result, scenario, or audience in natural searchable language.
- Opening: state the conclusion or practical gain immediately, then establish credible context.
- Structure: use short ordered blocks, a checklist, or a small number of clearly labeled steps; align the body with the image sequence.
- Voice: compact, direct, and personal only when the approved source contains real personal experience.
- Depth: prioritize decisions, steps, and pitfalls; move optional technical detail out of the main flow.
- Links: do not rely on clickable external links; provide exact product names, commands, or search terms when readers need to find something.
- Ending: offer one specific save, comment, or comparison prompt that adds to the discussion.

## Visuals

- Use a strong vertical cover that states the real content promise and a coherent sequence of phone-legible cards or screenshots.
- Keep each card focused on one idea and make card order match body order.
- Crop runtime screenshots for legibility without hiding the context needed to prove the feature.

## Avoid

- Emoji walls, hashtag stuffing, fake diary voice, fabricated personal results, absolute claims, and pasting a long-form article into the caption.
- Decorative cards that repeat the title without adding information.

## Platform QA

- Confirm title, first paragraph, cover, card sequence, and body make the same promise.
- Check that every claimed experience is sourced and every product demonstration remains authentic after cropping.

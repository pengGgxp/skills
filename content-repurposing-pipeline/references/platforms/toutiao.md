# Toutiao Platform Profile

Platform ID: `toutiao`

## Position

- Purpose: accessible technology explanation and practical experience for a broad audience.
- Reader intent: understand why a technology matters and whether a method or tool is useful in daily work.
- Best fit: AI tools, productivity, digital life, consumer technology, and broadly relevant technical stories.

## Write

- Title: express a concrete change, question, or useful result without exaggerating certainty or stakes.
- Opening: start from an everyday situation and explain the relevance before introducing specialist terms.
- Structure: relatable problem, plain-language explanation, method or example, evidence, limits, and takeaway.
- Voice: clear, lively, and respectful of non-specialists; define unavoidable jargon immediately.
- Depth: keep mechanisms that affect decisions, but move low-level implementation detail out of the main path.
- Links: use sparingly and describe the source or product clearly in the text.
- Ending: invite a grounded comparison or experience rather than generic engagement.

## Visuals

- Prefer recognizable scenarios, authentic product demonstrations, simple comparisons, and explanatory diagrams.
- Make captions understandable without technical background.

## Avoid

- Fear-based headlines, miracle claims, false dichotomies, unexplained jargon, and presenting a niche workflow as universal.
- Decorative technology imagery that does not prove or explain anything.

## Platform QA

- Confirm a general reader can follow the argument without losing the important caveats.
- Check that simplification has not turned correlation, opinion, or a single test into a fact.

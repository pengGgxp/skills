# Jianshu Platform Profile

Platform ID: `jianshu`

## Position

- Purpose: readable personal essays, experience notes, and approachable tutorials.
- Reader intent: follow a coherent story or learn a method without heavy technical framing.
- Best fit: reflective practice, learning notes, productivity, creative process, and accessible tutorials.

## Write

- Title: express the central experience, lesson, or practical promise in natural language.
- Opening: establish a real situation or question and the author's relationship to it.
- Structure: narrative context, turning point or problem, method, example, and reflective takeaway.
- Voice: personal, clear, and unforced; keep first-person claims faithful to the source.
- Depth: explain enough to act, while moving dense technical detail into concise examples or links.
- Links: keep only links that materially help the reader and introduce them in context.
- Ending: close the narrative or lesson without a promotional sales pitch.

## Visuals

- Use a restrained cover and a small number of meaningful illustrations, screenshots, or process images.
- Preserve a calm reading flow and avoid image walls.

## Avoid

- Invented personal stories, generic life lessons, ornamental prose, search-keyword stuffing, and copying a technical manual unchanged.
- Treating emotional resonance as evidence for a factual claim.

## Platform QA

- Confirm the narrative is true to the approved source and leads to a concrete insight.
- Check pacing, paragraph flow, technical accessibility, and the distinction between experience and general advice.

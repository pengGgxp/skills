# WeChat Official Account Platform Profile

Platform ID: `wechat`

## Position

- Purpose: sustained mobile reading, subscriber trust, sharing, and discussion.
- Reader intent: understand a useful experience or idea without leaving the article repeatedly.
- Best fit: practical experience, tutorials, product stories, methods, and considered opinions.

## Write

- Title: create truthful curiosity through a recognizable problem, tension, or outcome; avoid sensational promises.
- Opening: begin with a concrete scene, question, or conflict and establish the reading payoff in the first screen.
- Structure: move from situation to problem, method, evidence or example, and a concise takeaway.
- Voice: natural spoken Chinese, warm but restrained, with short paragraphs and explicit transitions.
- Depth: explain essential technical ideas while compressing details that interrupt mobile reading; preserve commands only when readers need to act.
- Links: do not make comprehension depend on external links; integrate attribution naturally and keep optional links secondary.
- Ending: close the argument, then use one modest prompt to follow, share, or discuss when appropriate.

## Visuals

- Prepare a platform-appropriate cover after checking current dimensions in the publishing surface.
- Place useful visuals at section transitions or claims, and inspect every caption and screenshot at phone width.
- Prefer a coherent visual sequence over a dense gallery.

## Avoid

- Artificial suspense, generic motivational openings, excessive bold text, long unbroken paragraphs, and a detached reference dump.
- Copying a technical Blog version without rebuilding its reading rhythm.

## Platform QA

- Read the entire version at phone width and check first-screen value, paragraph rhythm, section transitions, and ending tone.
- Confirm reduced technical detail did not remove evidence, prerequisites, or important limits.
